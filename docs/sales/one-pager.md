# CoachingDesk — one-page sales sheet

**For coaching institutes running batches.** Attendance with instant parent alerts, fee collection with
proper receipts, batch announcements, shared notes, and enquiry follow-up — in one place, on any phone or laptop.

## The four problems it removes

1. **"Parents don't know their child skipped class."**
   Teacher marks the batch present/absent in one screen. Parents of absent students get a message the same day.
2. **"Fee collection is a diary and a lot of phone calls."**
   Batch-wise fee plans, per-student discounts with a reason on record, cash/UPI/online payments, and a
   numbered receipt every time — `ABC/2627/00001`, restarting each financial year, so your CA stops complaining.
3. **"Announcements reach half the parents."**
   One message to the whole institute or to a single batch.
4. **"Enquiries get lost in a notebook."**
   Every enquiry from your landing page lands in a follow-up list with reminders, and converts to an
   admission in one click.

## What you also get

- **Your own branded admissions page** at a public link you can put in WhatsApp status and pamphlets.
- **Your own payment account.** Online fees go straight into *your* Razorpay account, not ours. We never hold your money.
- **Your data stays yours and stays separate.** Each institute's records are isolated; nightly backups with a tested restore.
- **Multi-branch and staff logins.** Owner, branch manager, teacher, accountant, receptionist — each sees only what they need.
- **Reports** including a defaulters list you can export to Excel, and bulk student import from your existing sheet.
- **Parent view** of attendance and dues.

## What it is not

Not a school ERP. No transport, hostel, library, payroll, board-exam or TC/migration handling.
No college semester structure. If you need those, we're the wrong fit and we'll say so.

## What we need from you to go live

1. Your Razorpay account keys (only if you want online fee payment).
2. A WhatsApp Business sender, or we start on SMS.
3. Parent consent to be messaged — we record it per guardian.
4. Your student list in any spreadsheet, and your batch/fee structure.

Setup takes minutes: we create your institute and hand you an owner login on the spot.

## Pilot offer (first 10 institutes)

Free for the first three months. In return: 15 minutes of feedback a week, a testimonial if you're happy,
and two referrals. After that, a flat monthly fee agreed up front — no lock-in, and your data is exportable.

If you stop, nothing is deleted. Your account is paused and everything is waiting when you come back.
